Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy describes the current Mac application, Tim’s Learn the Thai Alphabet, and this support website. It will be reviewed before an iPad release or any change to these practices.

Information in the app

The app does not require account registration or an email address. It has no advertising, subscriptions, in-app purchases, developer-operated analytics, or tracking tools.

The application does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. Your identity, learning history, quiz results, and settings are not automatically uploaded or sent to the developer.

Information stored on your device

The app saves learning history, progress and Learning Status, study goals, streaks, badges, and settings locally on your Mac. Dashboard statistics and Practice Tips are calculated locally. Storing these records for you is different from collecting them on a developer-operated service.

Pronunciation recordings are included with the app. Playing them does not contact Azure or another online speech service. Normal learning activities and built-in Help work offline.

Your backups, exports, and deletion choices

Settings → History provides controls to back up all app data, restore a backup, export history, and delete selected categories of learning data. You choose where exported files are saved. Restore replaces saved app data and settings; it does not merge histories.

Backups and exports are not encrypted by the app. A restore also creates a local recovery backup. Deleting data in the app does not delete backups or exports you have already saved or shared, or retained recovery backups.

If you save a file in a cloud-synced folder or send it to another person or service, that is your choice and that recipient’s privacy practices apply. The app does not share your exports automatically. See backup and restore guidance.

Support email

If you choose to email support using the address on the Support page, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you send. This is separate from data collection by the application. Send only the information needed to explain the issue; learning-history files are not required for an initial report. Email is handled by your email provider and the developer’s Gmail provider under their own privacy practices.

This website and external services

This website includes no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies set by the site, third-party fonts, or external JavaScript libraries. Its small local script displays the support email link. There is no contact form.

When published, the site will be hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process website request information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. This policy does not promise that GitHub collects no information.

Apple operates the App Store and processes its own account, download, and diagnostic information. Apple may provide developers with aggregate download statistics and other reports; diagnostic and usage sharing depends on Apple’s services and your settings. See App Store & Privacy.

Optional links take you to other websites, where their own privacy and cookie practices apply. External websites are not required for normal use of the app.

Questions

For privacy questions, use the protected email link on the Support page.
