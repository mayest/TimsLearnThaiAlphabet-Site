Learn at your own pace

Study Cards, Whack-a-Mole, Quizzes, and Reference Charts offer different ways to practice. Dashboard and History & Statistics help you review your activity. Learning records stay on your device.

The app is a companion for learning the writing system, rather than a complete Thai language course.

Free to use, with no account

No advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Study activities, recordings, charts, and built-in Help work offline.

Using more than one platform

You can use the apps independently, but learning data does not automatically sync. Backups are platform-specific: Windows backups cannot be restored on Apple devices, and Apple backups cannot be restored on Windows.
