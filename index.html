Tim’s Learn the Thai Alphabet

Build familiarity with Thai consonants, vowels, and numerals—one focused practice session at a time.

Study characters carefully, hear their names, compare similar shapes, and practice recognizing them in different Thai fonts. The app is a companion for learning the writing system, rather than a complete Thai language course.

Several ways to learn

Study Cards
Take your time with characters, explanations, and pronunciation.
Whack-a-Mole
Practice recognition with visual and listening games.
Quizzes
Check what you remember without speed pressure.
Reference Charts
Explore character relationships and look up details.

Local learning history, Dashboard reports, and optional study goals help you decide what to practice next.

Platform and release status

The Mac application targets Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Runtime compatibility with macOS 15 has not yet been verified. The app is not yet available in the Mac App Store.

An iPad version is planned. It is not yet available, and no release date has been announced.

Free to use

No account, advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Study activities, pronunciation recordings, reference charts, and built-in Help work offline.
