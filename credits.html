Credits & Licenses

The application combines original code and teaching material with third-party pronunciation references. The licenses for those references are separate from the application’s original code, artwork, recordings, and other content.

Wiktionary pronunciation data

Credit: Wiktionary contributors. Some IPA and Paiboon pronunciation transcriptions are sourced or adapted from the entries linked below and their contributor histories, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0). The documented source review was September 16, 2026.

The affected full-name fields are consonant ThaiNameIPA and ThaiNamePaiboon, vowel ThaiVowelNameIPA and ThaiVowelNamePaiboon, and number ThaiNumberNameIPA and ThaiNumberNamePaiboon.

Modifications include normalization of notation and separators, composition of full vowel names from lexical components and Thai spelling rules, and composition of number names from their existing Thai-name components. These are citation-form teaching transcriptions, not acoustic transcriptions of the bundled recordings. Composite names are adaptations, not claims that each complete name is a separate dictionary entry.

Consonant InitialPaiboon and FinalPaiboon represent source IPA phonemes in the project’s Paiboon+ convention, checked against Wiktionary’s romanization table and Slice of Thai. Paiboon+ distinguishes short and long diphthongs. No publisher or contributor endorsement is implied.

Other reference acknowledgments

  • Wiktionary’s Thai romanization table and Slice of Thai’s pronunciation guides: convention references for Paiboon+ notation.
  • UNGEGN Thai romanization tables and Royal Institute transcription rules hosted by Chulalongkorn University: references for RTGS correspondence.
  • Wikipedia’s Thai script reference and Thai numerals reference, by Wikipedia contributors: sources cited for naming, spelling, and number-component conventions. Wikimedia text reuse is subject to the linked Wikimedia terms; any copied or adapted protectable text requires its own attribution and license treatment.

The provenance does not establish that every IPA, RTGS, TLTA, vowel-pattern/example transcription, or audio recording was copied from Wiktionary. A separate per-example citation ledger has not been documented. These acknowledgments do not grant new permissions for material whose rights have not been established.

Recorded pronunciations

Thanks to Wassana (Emmy), a native Thai speaker, for recording the names, demonstration patterns, and available example words for เ◌ียะ, เ◌ือะ, and ◌ัวะ. These replace synthesized versions that did not reliably convey the short sounds.

Other bundled speech was prepared during development, including with Azure Speech. Playing recordings in the app does not contact an online speech service. The recordings are not covered by the Wiktionary transcription license merely because they pronounce the same words.

Licensing review before distribution

The boundaries of protectable transcription adaptations, composed fields, database rights, and Apple distribution terms still need review. Permissions for contributed and synthesized audio and other supplied artwork also need confirmation before public app distribution.

No application dataset download is provided on this site. A separately approved, data-only download with field-level attribution and license notices is a practical option for licensed adaptations; it would not require publishing the private application repository. That option does not by itself settle App Store license compatibility.
