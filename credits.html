The affected full-name fields are consonant ThaiNameIPA and ThaiNamePaiboon, vowel ThaiVowelNameIPA and ThaiVowelNamePaiboon, and number ThaiNumberNameIPA and ThaiNumberNamePaiboon.

Modifications include normalization of notation and separators, composition of full vowel names from lexical components and Thai spelling rules, and composition of number names from their existing Thai-name components. These are citation-form teaching transcriptions, not acoustic transcriptions of the bundled recordings. Composite names are adaptations, not claims that each complete name is a separate dictionary entry.

Consonant InitialPaiboon and FinalPaiboon represent source IPA phonemes in the project’s Paiboon+ convention, checked against Wiktionary’s romanization table and Slice of Thai. Paiboon+ distinguishes short and long diphthongs. No publisher or contributor endorsement is implied.
