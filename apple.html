Tim’s Learn the Thai Alphabet

Build familiarity with Thai consonants, vowels, and numerals—one focused practice session at a time.

Study characters carefully, hear their names, compare similar shapes, and practice recognizing them in different Thai fonts. The app is a companion for learning the writing system, rather than a complete Thai language course.

Choose the pronunciation guide that works best for you. Switch between four systems: TLTA (a custom, American English-friendly guide designed for this app), RTGS, IPA, and Paiboon.

Several ways to learn

Screenshots show the Mac version. Select a screenshot to enlarge it.

Study Cards

Learn Thai characters at your own pace.

Explore all 44 Thai consonants, 32 traditional vowel forms, and 290 Thai numbers. Reveal each card for pronunciation recordings and useful details. Optional hints help with unfamiliar items, while Practice More and Know It let you choose what to review.

Whack-a-Mole

Make learning the Thai alphabet into a game.

Practice recognizing Thai characters and numbers on grids from 2×2 to 6×6, where supported. Choose visual or listening exercises, compare similar-looking forms, and practice consonant classes and vowel lengths. Try different Thai fonts, track your accuracy, and compare personal-best times for matching game setups.

Quizzes

Find out how much you remember.

Choose multiple-choice questions about consonants, vowels, numbers, and recorded pronunciations. Answer without speed pressure and review mistakes afterward. When enough practice history is available, Focus on trouble spots gives areas of difficulty more attention.

Reference Charts

Keep useful Thai language references close at hand.

Browse charts of Thai consonants, the 32 traditional vowel forms, and selected number sets. Explore consonant classes, sound relationships, and visually similar forms. Select items for further details and available pronunciation recordings. On Mac, print charts or save them as PDF files.

See how you’re progressing

Track completed games, Quiz results, Study Card activity, and Learning Status in your personal Dashboard. Review accuracy, find items that need more attention, and set optional daily or weekly study goals. Learning information is stored on your device.

Platform and release status

The Mac application targets Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Runtime compatibility with macOS 15 has not yet been verified. The app is not yet available in the Mac App Store.

An iPad version is planned. It is not yet available, and no release date has been announced.

Free to use

No account, advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Study activities, pronunciation recordings, reference charts, and built-in Help work offline.
